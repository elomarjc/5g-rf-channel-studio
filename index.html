📡 5G/6G RF Physical-Layer Channel & Constellation Studio

🎯 The Industrial Problem Solved

Designing next-generation 5G NR and 6G radios requires transceivers to maintain high modulation purity (EVM < 3.5% for 256-QAM) while operating through hostile wireless environments: high Doppler shifts (from high-speed trains at 300+ km/h), multi-path Rayleigh/Rician fading from buildings, local oscillator phase noise, and mixer I/Q imbalances. Hardware RF testing without million-dollar anechoic chamber testbeds is expensive and slow.

💡 How This Digital Twin Solves It

This digital twin emulates a full 3GPP TS 38.104 physical-layer testbench directly in software: baseband I/Q modulation (BPSK to 256-QAM), 5G NR 14-symbol × 24-subcarrier OFDM resource grids, Clarke/Jakes Doppler spectrums, and real-time Monte Carlo Bit Error Rate (BER) sweeps benchmarked against closed-form theoretical AWGN error curves.

🕹️ Interactive Quickstart Tutorial (Try This Now!)

📊 Telemetry & Control Glossary

  • EVM (Error Vector Magnitude): Measures modulation accuracy between ideal and measured I/Q constellation symbols; 3GPP specifies <3.5% for 256-QAM and <8% for 64-QAM.
  • Phosphor Persistence Scope: Simulates CRT analog phosphor decay to visualize transient phase noise and multi-path inter-symbol interference in real time.
  • OFDM Resource Grid: Maps 14 time symbols across 24 frequency subcarriers representing 5G NR Physical Resource Blocks (PRBs).
