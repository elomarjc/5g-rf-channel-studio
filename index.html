5G NR Physical Layer RF Studio — Engineering Whitepaper

1. 5G NR Numerology & Constellation Mapping

Simulates 3GPP Release 16/17 physical downlink shared channel (PDSCH) modulation mappings: QPSK, 16-QAM, 64-QAM, and 256-QAM with Gray constellation bit labeling.

2. Multipath Fading & Statistical Channel Profiles

Emulates tapped-delay-line mobile channels with classical Clarke/Jakes Doppler spectra: Rayleigh (non-line-of-sight urban multipath) and Rician (line-of-sight specular path with configurable K-factor).

3. RF Front-End Impairments & 3GPP TS 38.104 EVM Conformance

Models analog hardware non-idealities: Phase noise (Wiener random walk process), I/Q gain imbalance (±3 dB), quadrature phase skew (±15°), and DC carrier leakage. Computes RMS Error Vector Magnitude (EVM) evaluated strictly against 3GPP conformance thresholds (QPSK ≤ 17.5%, 16-QAM ≤ 12.5%, 64-QAM ≤ 8.0%, 256-QAM ≤ 3.5%).
